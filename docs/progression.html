Observations

Antonelli's Trajectory
After finishing P2 in Australia, Antonelli has won three consecutive races. His 93-point haul from Rounds 2-4 is the highest three-race total on the grid. At 19, he's on pace for a record-breaking maiden championship — projected at ~600 points over 24 races if current form holds.
McLaren's Recovery
McLaren had a disastrous Round 2 with both drivers DNS. Since then, Piastri and Norris have combined for 61 points in two races (Rounds 3-4), the highest two-race team total behind Mercedes. Their trajectory suggests they're the strongest challenger once reliability stabilizes.
Red Bull's Decline
Verstappen sits 7th with 26 points — his worst start to a season since his debut year. He retired in China and has failed to finish on the podium. Red Bull's estimated 10 kg weight surplus correlates with their ~0.3-0.4s per lap deficit to Mercedes.
The Reliability Factor
The 2026 regulations have brought a wave of unreliability. Of 88 race starts across 4 rounds, there have been 14 retirements and 4 DNS entries. Aston Martin leads the misery with zero finishes in the points and multiple retirements. The new power unit architecture is taking its toll.
