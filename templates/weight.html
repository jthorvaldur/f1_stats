Driver Weight Advantage
The FIA sets a minimum driver+seat weight of {{ data.min_driver_weight }} kg. Drivers under this carry ballast placed low in the chassis, effectively lowering the center of gravity. {% if lightest_driver and heaviest_driver %}{{ lightest_driver.family_name }} ({{ lightest_driver.weight_kg }} kg, ~{{ lightest_driver.ballast }} kg ballast) gets a marginal CoG benefit over {{ heaviest_driver.family_name }} ({{ heaviest_driver.weight_kg }} kg, ~{{ heaviest_driver.ballast }} kg ballast).{% endif %}
Points vs. Car Weight
The correlation between car weight and constructor points is notable. Lighter cars tend to perform better — weight is not destiny, but in the tight midfield it can be the difference between points and nothing.
